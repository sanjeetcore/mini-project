ABOUT COLLEGE

GNIT IPU, Grade 'A' GGSIPU affiliated College in Greater Noida, offers industry-focused undergraduate programs including BBA,
BCA, and B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering (CSE) and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML). Situated in Knowledge
Park II, GNIT IPU is backed by highly qualified faculty from IITs and NITs, modern infrastructure, and dedicated placement support.
Committed to nurturing skilled, industry-ready professionals, the institute blends strong academic foundations with practical
exposure, innovation, and holistic development, shaping confident graduates for successful careers ahead.
